Black Eagle VPN connects your Android device to the Black Eagle VPN service using AmneziaWG.

Import an activation link from the Telegram bot, select a server and connect. The app includes server latency checks, favourites, subscription information, per-app split tunnelling, light and dark themes, and Russian and English interfaces.

Access requires an active Black Eagle subscription or trial. Account activation uses Telegram at https://t.me/BlackEagleVPNbot. This app is tied to the Black Eagle service at https://blackeagle-vpn.com/ and is not a general-purpose client for arbitrary VPN providers.

Requires Android 11 or later. VPN protection applies while the VPN is connected; apps excluded by split tunnelling use their normal connection.
